# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Readers, researchers, and technical practitioners who explore a body of AI,
large-model systems, and security knowledge by topic rather than by publication
date. They may arrive to discover a problem area, follow a concept across
articles, or spend a long uninterrupted session reading a scholarly post.

## Product Purpose

MyBlog is a theme-first knowledge blog. It organizes posts, topics, and concepts
as a navigable knowledge network, then provides a reading runtime that keeps
navigation, references, marginalia, and whole-article discussion available
without breaking the main narrative. Success means readers can find the right
problem domain quickly and stay oriented through a long technical article.

## Brand Personality

Calm, research-minded, and quietly opinionated. The site should feel like a
carefully maintained technical notebook with atmospheric depth: clear enough
for sustained reading, distinctive enough to feel authored, and restrained
enough that interface chrome never competes with the content.

## Anti-references

- A generic reverse-chronological blog where publication date is the primary
  navigation model.
- A high-saturation technology demo whose glow, animation, or glass effects
  become the main subject.
- A conventional article template that collapses the scholarly reading runtime
  into body copy plus a generic sticky table of contents.
- Carousel-advertising motion, repeated reveal effects, or micro-interactions
  that draw attention away from reading.
- Pure-black dark mode, flat unmodulated cards, and unrelated visual systems
  for discover and reading routes.

## Design Principles

1. **Topic first.** Lead with problem domains and conceptual relationships,
   not a time feed.
2. **Reading is the primary task.** Navigation and tools stay available but
   defer to the article.
3. **Separate information by role.** Main narrative, TOC, marginalia,
   references, and comments each have a clear place.
4. **Motion explains state.** Movement should make spatial and interaction
   changes legible; decorative motion is removed.
5. **Preserve authored identity.** Discover and reading runtimes can differ in
   density while remaining one visual family.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, keyboard-accessible navigation and search, visible
focus states, semantic landmarks, and stable content without hydration. Honor
`prefers-reduced-motion` for every transition and animation; reduced-motion
users must receive the complete interface with state changes applied
immediately rather than an animation-dependent substitute.
