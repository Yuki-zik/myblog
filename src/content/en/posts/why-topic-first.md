A blog organized only by publication date can quickly leave readers lost in its timeline.

## The value of a topic-first approach

Topic pages make natural entry points because they express a problem domain directly, rather than a publication date.

When an article becomes a node in a network of topics, older writing can keep finding new relevance.

## How to avoid overengineering

The first version only needs clear topic boundaries, introductory articles, and related concepts. A complex graph can wait.

Once enough content has accumulated, consider adding a visualization of backlinks.
